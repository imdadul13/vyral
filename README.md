# VYRAL — The AI Operating System for Viral Content

**Make Every Moment Count.**  
*Discover. Clip. Create. Go Viral.*

VYRAL is an end-to-end AI video intelligence, short-form clipping, kinetic subtitle rendering, and multi-platform publishing studio built with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Gemini 3.8 Flash & Gemini 4 Argon, PostgreSQL/Supabase, and FFmpeg 9.0.

## Key Capabilities

1. **AI YouTube Video Clip Generator & 10-Dimension Viral Moment Engine**
2. **VYRAL Viral Optimizer & Dedicated 9-Section Publishing Studio**
   - Overview & 8-Pillar Publishing Optimization Score (0–100)
   - AI Viral Title Generator (10+ styles, side-by-side comparison, inline editing)
   - AI Description Generator (Short & Punchy, SEO Optimized, Conversational)
   - Smart Hashtag Generator (Verified Snapshot Trends vs AI Topic Matches)
   - YouTube SEO Tags Generator (with live 500-character limit validator)
   - Viral Hook Optimizer (0–5s spoken transcript analysis + 5 visual hook overlays)
   - AI Thumbnail & Cover Optimizer (9:16 Shorts & 16:9 Long-form + keyframe detection)
   - Pinned Comment Generator (5 discussion-first styles)
   - Multi-Platform Interactive Preview (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Long-form)
3. **Advanced AI Subtitle Studio (9 Presets) & Smart Video Editor**
4. **Trending Now (Periodic Snapshot Velocity Engine) & Viral Radar**
5. **Server-Side FFmpeg 9.0 Hardware-Accelerated MP4 Rendering**
